# UIR legal research

Derived legal-domain project, reusing the shared core and explicit base interfaces
without copying them. Project-wide design belongs in `docs/`, reusable inputs in
`resources/`, and evaluation/orchestration sources in `evaluation/` and `scripts/`.
Manuscript workspaces are local/private under `paper/`. Unapproved corpora are not public.

Keep every task's data, reports, figures, resources and logs together in
`results/<task_id>/`. Private work orders/reviews/work reports stay at root
`docs/work_reports/uir_law/<task_id>/`, LOCAL ONLY.
See [repository layout](../../docs/architecture/PROJECT_LAYOUT.md).
