# Contribution decision

The defensible candidate contribution is an auditable composition of typed
request, policy snapshot, evidence binding, and explicit accepted-answer states.
It is not the invention of IRs, CNL, policy engines, constrained decoding, or
runtime agent enforcement. Each proposed benefit needs its own test.

Historical same-fact trusted-slot comparison: deterministic template obtains
272/418 complete answers versus UIR's 224/418 complete plus 48 partial; there
are 48 deterministic-only complete cases and no UIR-only complete cases. Both
cover 272 supported answers. This does not support a necessary or accuracy-
improving SLM renderer. The old C5+projection replay is separately labeled and
the all-candidate replay does not show unique UIR groundedness superiority.

The local Q4_0 experiment is a *new development condition*, not a rerun of the
old FP16/NF4 backend. Preserve its negative result. Its historical schema
requires five fields whereas the exact scorer binds seven. Optional unit/period
loss therefore prevents interpreting the resulting 0/418 complete score as a
general Phi model incapability or a causal estimate of typed-IR contribution.

Keep historical external negative results, all missing output labels and low
AI-review agreement visible. Synthetic reviewer conformance is not human gold.
Original W08 is NOT_SATISFIED under user-approved AI-only amendment B.

Allowed: supported CNL conformance, evidence-authority boundaries, explicit
failure states, recorded fixture action effects, reproducible rescoring.
Not established: human semantic accuracy, general NL robustness, domain-wide
truth, independent AI errors, superior utility/speed, causal ablation attribution,
host/registry-compromise resistance, or readiness for publication.
