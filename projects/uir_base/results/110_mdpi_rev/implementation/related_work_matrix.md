# Direct-source positioning check (2026-10-12 KST)

Sources were opened directly; bibliographic metadata below is not inferred from
the work order. This is a mechanism comparison, not an empirical reproduction of
the named systems. Our C5/C6/C7 conditions are mechanism-matched implementations.

| Direct source | Already possible there | UIR addition being evaluated | Evidence here / remaining limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [CaMeL, revised arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18813v2) | Trusted control/data separation and capability-governed tool calls | Common policy/evidence/output contract and structured claim projection | Runtime-boundary negative tests and sandbox traces; not a CaMeL or AgentDojo reproduction |
| [AgentSpec, ICSE 2026 author-hosted final paper](https://cposkitt.github.io/files/publications/agentspec_llm_enforcement_icse26.pdf) | Trigger/predicate/enforcement DSL, runtime action restrictions | Evidence-bound answer states with canonical multilingual contract identity | Restricted CNL/state conformance; no novelty claim for DSL enforcement itself; DOI 10.1145/3744916.3764546 |
| [Open Policy Agent / Rego](https://www.openpolicyagent.org/docs/policy-language) | Declarative policy decisions over structured context | Policy snapshot identity bound to an evidence/output contract | Snapshot mismatch/approval revocation traces; no claim of inventing policy engines |
| [Kuhn, Controlled Natural Languages, CL 2014](https://aclanthology.org/J14-1005/) | Restricted, formally interpretable language interfaces | Shared bilingual frontend to a narrower executable contract | Recursive AST/digest tests; no free-NL parser accuracy or human usability validation |
| [XGrammar, original full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.15100v1) | Grammar-based structured token generation | Runtime factual/policy binding beyond syntactic admissibility | Historical D1 schema versus exact-field score; GGUF/Ollama does not imply an XGrammar reproduction |
| [ChopChop, PACMPL 10 POPL article 66](https://doi.org/10.1145/3776708) | Semantic pruners over AST/program spaces during constrained generation | Post-generation evidence/permission binding for financial fixtures | A specific contract composition, not the first semantic constraint approach; publisher record dated 2026-01-08 |

The methods address different tasks. No paper's headline score or speed is directly
comparable to the constructed financial cohort. We therefore do not import their
claims as evidence for UIR's accuracy, overhead, or security guarantees.

“Universal” here means one representation shared by supported frontends, not
general natural-language semantics or objective correctness of a compromised
fact registry. Safety means specified runtime conformance under trusted policy,
registry, and executor assumptions, not arbitrary prompt-injection immunity.
